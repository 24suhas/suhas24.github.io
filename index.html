Industrial Visit to Tata Motors – A Learning Experience

As part of our MBA journey at S.B. Patil Institute of Management, we had the incredible opportunity to visit the Tata Motors Passenger Vehicle Plant. Witnessing the production of Safari and Harrier up close gave us valuable insights into the automotive industry's technology, processes, and management strategies.

One of the most inspiring moments was seeing the dedication of women on the assembly line, highlighting the power of diversity in the industry. This visit truly bridged the gap between classroom learning and real-world applications.

A big thank you to Tata Motors for this eye-opening experience!
